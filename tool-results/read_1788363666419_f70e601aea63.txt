   127→
   128→ADDENDUM — asset-class risk calibration: crypto is not stocks with a different ticker. When the crypto segment is re-added, SENTINEL's risk engine must use asset-class-specific parameters (volatility bands, position sizing, stop distance in ATR terms, and daily-loss circuit breakers) for crypto rather than reusing equity defaults — 24/7 markets and higher realized volatility will otherwise blow through equity-calibrated risk limits and either trigger constant circuit breakers or, worse, undersize the risk controls for how fast crypto moves.
