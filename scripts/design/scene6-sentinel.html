Automation with a governor
SENTINEL is a second pair of hands, not an autopilot. Approve keeps a human in every loop; Delegate still lives inside rails you set.
Observe mode first
New sessions start watching, not acting. SENTINEL earns execution rights one approval at a time.
Paper broker, real discipline
Fills are simulated — the discipline is not. Every draft, approval, rejection and halt is written to the immutable audit trail.
