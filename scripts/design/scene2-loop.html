How everything works — the DeeYoung loop
read → investigate → decide → act → learn
STEP 1
Read the regime
The engine classifies the tape — momentum, chop, or stress — with a confidence number. Thresholds shift with the market instead of fixed guesses.
DASHBOARD · REGIME GAUGE
STEP 2
Spot the setup
Seven factors score every symbol — EMA structure, VWAP, RSI, MACD, catalysts, regime and more. The math behind each score is on the card, never hidden.
SIGNALS · SCORE RINGS
STEP 3
Check the risk
Before you act: concentration, correlation, scenario shocks (-5%, -10%, vol spike) and drawdown — risk beyond P&L, on one panel.
PORTFOLIO · RISK COMPOSITE
STEP 4
Make the call
Ask the desk bot in plain English — it returns direction, entry, stop, two targets, R:R and what kills the idea, grounded in live prices.
TRADE DESK · AI ANALYST
STEP 5
Let SENTINEL draft — you approve
Pro can draft risk-checked trades for one-tap approval, or delegate inside hard limits you set. The bot proposes; nothing executes without you.

Flat is a position
If nothing clears your threshold, the terminal says so plainly: "Nothing above 55 in this filter." The scan reruns every minute; cards appear only when factor alignment genuinely clears the bar.
Honesty is a feature
Missing news key? The panel says NEWS DATA UNAVAILABLE — DeeYoung never displays invented news. Stale tape? A STALE chip flags it. Market closed? Orders are rejected, not faked, and the rejection is logged to the audit trail.
