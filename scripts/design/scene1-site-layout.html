1 · Promise before product
The hero states the whole value in one breath, and the amber strip immediately tells the truth about delayed data and paper-only execution. Trust is the layout.
2 · Try without an account
"Open the terminal — it's free" drops visitors into the live terminal with delayed data and a paper portfolio. No email wall in front of the product.
3 · Proof, not adjectives
The preview strip streams the same quotes the terminal uses. Four badges quantify the offer: 26 markets, 7 factors, 4 safety levels, 100% paper.
4 · Four pillars = four tabs
Each pillar maps 1:1 to a terminal area: Signals, Catalysts, Portfolio, SENTINEL. The landing teaches the app before you ever sign up.
5 · Pricing with no mystery tiers
Three plans, every line explicit, 2-day trial with no card. Currency switcher shows fair Naira reference prices.
6 · Legal that reads like legal
Footer carries ToS, Privacy, Security, Refunds — and the standing disclaimer that this is simulated execution, never a real brokerage.
