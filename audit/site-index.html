REALITY CHECK

What this bot will do for you — and what it will never do

Anyone promising 99.9% wins is selling you something. Markets are probabilistic; even elite desks run 45–60% win rates and stay profitable through asymmetric payoffs. What SENTINEL gives you is the part that actually compounds: consistency.

It will
  • · Apply the same 7-factor discipline to every scan, every day
  • · Risk a fixed fraction per trade and cap the daily damage
  • · Cut losers at -1.5 ATR without hesitating or hoping
  • · Show you exactly why it acted — every factor, every time
It will not
  • · Win every trade — losing streaks are mathematically guaranteed
  • · Trade with real money, or touch your brokerage account
  • · Front-run news or predict gap direction from thin air
  • · Replace your judgment — it disciplines it
