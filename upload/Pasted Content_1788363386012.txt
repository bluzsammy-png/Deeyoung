4. BRAIN 2 — SENTINEL

SENTINEL is the optional action layer sitting on top of Analytics.

It answers:

"Given what Analytics discovered, should I take action?"

SENTINEL receives opportunities/signals from Analytics and then applies:

* User permissions
* Risk limits
* Position limits
* Portfolio exposure
* Correlation limits
* Session restrictions
* Asset restrictions
* Strategy restrictions
* Maximum daily loss
* Maximum trade risk
* Maximum notional
* Maximum open positions
* Minimum liquidity
* Spread requirements
* Volatility restrictions
* News/event restrictions

Only after passing deterministic safety checks can SENTINEL proceed.

ADDENDUM — asset-class risk calibration: crypto is not stocks with a different ticker. When the crypto segment is re-added, SENTINEL's risk engine must use asset-class-specific parameters (volatility bands, position sizing, stop distance in ATR terms, and daily-loss circuit breakers) for crypto rather than reusing equity defaults — 24/7 markets and higher realized volatility will otherwise blow through equity-calibrated risk limits and either trigger constant circuit breakers or, worse, undersize the risk controls for how fast crypto moves.

55. SYSTEM FAILURE PRINCIPLE

QuantEdge must fail safely.

When uncertain:

DO NOTHING.

Never:

* Invent data
* Invent news
* Execute without permission
* Continue automation with stale data
* Ignore risk limits
* Assume broker connectivity
* Pretend an order filled
* Pretend a notification was delivered

70. MARKETING SITE / LAUNCH READINESS ADDENDUM

Findings specific to the current live marketing site (https://c1eek7j3be20-d.space-z.ai) that should be treated as part of the audit in section 62, not deferred to Phase 10:

* No product screenshot, chart preview, or example SENTINEL approval card anywhere on the page — the entire pitch is text. For a paid product, this is the highest-leverage fix, ahead of any font or color work (see addendum to section 37).
* No visible Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, or refund/cancellation policy, and no contact/company info — required before charging anyone, and required by both app stores at review (see addendum to section 51).
* "LIVE US EQUITIES · REAL-TIME DATA" is prominent in the hero; the "delayed per exchange terms" disclosure is footer-only. Surface it earlier (see addendum to section 50).
* Subscription billing plan needs to be decided before store submission: native apps selling digital subscriptions must use Apple's/Google's own in-app purchase, not a Stripe checkout embedded in the native app (see addendum to section 35).
* The news/catalyst feed (upgrade item ①) is the first feature in the roadmap with real, ongoing per-request cost — price it out against real provider limits before committing to it, not after (see addendum to section 11).
* Re-adding crypto (upgrade item ②) needs its own risk-engine calibration, not equity defaults reused for a 24/7, higher-volatility asset class (see addendum to section 4).
* Alpaca paper connectivity (upgrade item ③) should be built as a genuine on-ramp in the ExecutionProvider abstraction, with the live-trading regulatory implications flagged explicitly rather than assumed away (see addendum to section 19).
* Push notifications (upgrade item ④) can only reach a phone once a real iOS/Android app shell exists — sequence web push and email first, mobile push once Phase 9 produces an installable app (see addendum to section 25).
